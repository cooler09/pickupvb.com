## What shipped

**(a) [docs/adr/0009-canonical-domain-apex.md](../adr/0009-canonical-domain-apex.md).**

Records the decision that `pickupvb.com` (bare apex, no `www.`) is
canonical. Covers:

- The four places the decision is encoded today
  ([app-url.ts](../../apps/web/src/lib/app-url.ts) `PROD_APP_URL`,
  [robots.ts](../../apps/web/src/app/robots.ts),
  [sitemap.ts](../../apps/web/src/app/sitemap.ts), the JSON-LD `@id`
  pins) and the trust-boundary allow-list in
  [external-url.ts](../../packages/domain/src/community-listings/external-url.ts).
- The `PROD_APP_URL` / `APP_URL` / `IS_PROD_HOST` split: SEO-canonical
  identifiers pinned to prod, per-visitor share links following the
  current deployment, `IS_PROD_HOST` gating real `robots.txt` +
  `sitemap.xml`.
- `dev.pickupvb.com` as the staging form (cross-link to
  [docs/environments.md](../environments.md)).
- Trade-offs: apex DNS needs ANAME / ALIAS-style records (which
  Vercel handles natively); cookies set on apex are visible to any
  future subdomain (mitigated by scoping auth cookies to apex only).
- Three rejected alternatives: `www.` as canonical, serve both
  equally, and a different staging apex (`pickupvb-dev.com`).

The original audit and the 2026-05-17 status block called this
"ADR 0006 (canonical-domain-apex)". Slots 0006–0008 have since been
taken by `event-divisions`, `team-registration-model`, and
`team-registration-paradigm`, so the apex ADR moved to the next free
number — **0009**. [docs/adr/README.md](../adr/README.md) index updated.

## Patterns surfaced

- **Audit findings can be already-fixed in the tree but never closed
  in the log.** Worth a quick re-read of every open finding when
  picking the next bundle — at least two of the docs P2s required no
  code change, just a flip. The "audits index" one in particular was
  self-referential: it was effectively closed the moment the audit was
  written.
- **ADR slot drift.** If an audit names a specific ADR number, that
  number can be taken by a concurrent decision before the backfill
  happens. The right move is to take the next free slot and note the
  rename in the remediation log, not to renumber existing accepted
  ADRs (ADRs are immutable per the index header). Mention both the
  promised number and the final number so anyone searching old chat
  for "0006-canonical-domain-apex" still finds it.
- **Encoded-but-undocumented decisions are common.** The apex decision
  was present in five separate files (`app-url.ts`, `robots.ts`,
  `sitemap.ts`, `external-url.ts`, `environments.md`) but the
  rationale lived only in chat. The ADR pulls the rationale into one
  place and cross-links to all the encoded locations. Same shape will
  apply to the OpenInNewTab and Stripe-webhook-dedupe backfills.
